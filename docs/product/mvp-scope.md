# MVP scope

Status: authoritative  
Last reviewed: 2026-08-01

CorridorShare connects a sender with a traveler whose planned route passes close to a parcel pickup point. Both website and mobile are first-class clients of the same Supabase backend.

## Included

- Phone-based user sessions and member profiles.
- Traveler trip creation with a route and capacity.
- Sender package requests with pickup/drop-off locations and reward.
- Corridor matching with bounded near-miss distance.
- Participant-only deal chat and inspection evidence.
- Wallet hold, refund, and delivery payout recorded in an immutable ledger.
- Manual NID review by authorized administrators.
- Explicit demo mode for development and presentations.

## Excluded from the current MVP

- Nationwide logistics optimization.
- Platform-backed parcel insurance.
- Automated NID or facial recognition.
- Production payment credit without a confirmed provider callback.
- Background live-location tracking.
- Desktop Flutter releases.

Legal, payment-provider, privacy, and NID-retention policies require business approval before public launch.
